# Technology & Platforms

<p class="cf-lede">What technology and platforms does it rely upon?</p>

Use this lens when the domain could materially change the decision, the experiment, the risk or the ability to scale.

## Questions to ask

- What existing platforms can we use?
- What constraints are real?
- What deployment and hosting model fits the experiment?
- What choices could become expensive to reverse?

## Apply proportionately

Do not solve every future concern up front. Identify what matters to the decision now, what could invalidate the experiment, and what can safely wait until the evidence justifies greater commitment.
