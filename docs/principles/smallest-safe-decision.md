# Make the smallest safe decision

<p class="cf-lede">Decide only what we need to decide now.</p>

<div class="cf-panel cf-panel--principle"><strong>Principle</strong>Decide only what we need to decide now.</div>

## Why this matters

Large decisions made early are often based on assumptions rather than evidence. Prefer incremental and reversible choices that preserve the ability to learn and change.

## What this means in practice

Use this principle to make the decision smaller, clearer and easier to revisit as evidence changes.

## Questions to ask

- What decision actually needs to be made now?
- What can safely be deferred?
- Which parts are reversible?
- What evidence would allow the next decision?
- What would make changing direction expensive?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Do enough architecture to make the experiment safe and useful. Do not design the five-year target architecture unless the experiment genuinely requires it.

## Watch out for

- Premature target-state design
- Locking in strategic technology before testing the proposition
- Treating an experiment as the first release of the final solution
