# Use what exists — until it stops helping

<p class="cf-lede">Respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.</p>

<div class="cf-panel cf-panel--principle"><strong>Principle</strong>Respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.</div>

## Why this matters

Existing environments, platforms, standards and processes can reduce cost and support sustainable ownership. Reuse is valuable when it helps the outcome — it is not an absolute.

## What this means in practice

Use this principle to make the decision smaller, clearer and easier to revisit as evidence changes.

## Questions to ask

- What already exists that is genuinely suitable?
- What constraint is creating delay or preventing learning?
- Is the constraint technical, contractual, organisational or assumed?
- What would be required to depart safely?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Start within the partner environment where practical. If an existing constraint materially prevents the learning we need, make the constraint explicit and test or escalate it deliberately.

## Watch out for

- Bypassing governance by default
- Accepting every existing constraint without challenge
- Creating a CustomerFirst dependency that the partner cannot sustain
