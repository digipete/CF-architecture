# Prove rather than predict

<p class="cf-lede">Use evidence, experiments and observable behaviour rather than architectural opinion.</p>

<div class="cf-panel cf-panel--principle"><strong>Principle</strong>Use evidence, experiments and observable behaviour rather than architectural opinion.</div>

## Why this matters

Where uncertainty can be tested cheaply, test it. Prototypes, spikes, measurements and observed behaviour often produce better decisions than prolonged debate.

## What this means in practice

Use this principle to make the decision smaller, clearer and easier to revisit as evidence changes.

## Questions to ask

- Which assumption matters to this decision?
- Can we test it directly?
- What evidence would change our mind?
- What are we currently treating as fact without evidence?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Turn material architectural uncertainty into part of the experiment. Make the evidence visible and use it to support the next decision.

## Watch out for

- Architecture by assertion
- Debating testable questions
- Collecting evidence that cannot change the decision
