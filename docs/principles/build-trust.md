# Build trust into the design

<p class="cf-lede">Security, privacy, accessibility, resilience and operability are designed in rather than checked at the end.</p>

<div class="cf-panel cf-panel--principle"><strong>Principle</strong>Security, privacy, accessibility, resilience and operability are designed in rather than checked at the end.</div>

## Why this matters

Trust is a property of the service, not a final approval activity. Relevant specialists should influence the design early enough to change it.

## What this means in practice

Use this principle to make the decision smaller, clearer and easier to revisit as evidence changes.

## Questions to ask

- What could cause harm?
- What level of exposure are we creating?
- What failure modes matter here?
- What controls are proportionate now?
- What would need to strengthen before exposure increases?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

A bounded experiment does not need every control required by a national live service. It does need controls proportionate to its real exposure and consequence.

## Watch out for

- Using “experiment” as a reason to ignore risk
- Applying full production controls to low-risk learning
- Leaving accessibility or operability until the end
