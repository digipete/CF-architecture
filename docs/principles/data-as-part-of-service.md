# Treat data as part of the service

<p class="cf-lede">Understand data ownership, movement, quality, semantics and value from the start.</p>

<div class="cf-panel cf-panel--principle"><strong>Principle</strong>Understand data ownership, movement, quality, semantics and value from the start.</div>

## Why this matters

Data is not an implementation detail to resolve after the service is designed. The service, decisions, integrations and future uses all depend on understanding the information involved.

## What this means in practice

Use this principle to make the decision smaller, clearer and easier to revisit as evidence changes.

## Questions to ask

- What data is needed and why?
- Who owns it?
- How trustworthy is it?
- What does it mean in context?
- Where does it move?
- What sensitivity or retention constraints apply?

## During a Test & Learn Experiment

Use the experiment to validate data availability, quality and meaning when these could invalidate the proposition. Synthetic data may be enough for one question; real data may be essential for another.

## Watch out for

- Creating a new silo for convenience
- Assuming data quality
- Collecting data without a clear purpose
