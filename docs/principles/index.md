# Principles

<p class="cf-lede">How we think about technology and architectural decisions.</p>

These are not rules for architects. They are **decision-making tools for teams**.

<div class="cf-inset cf-inset--blue"><strong>Don't make a bigger decision than the evidence allows.</strong></div>

<div class="cf-grid">
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 01 — [Start with the outcome](start-with-the-outcome.md)
Understand the problem before choosing the technology.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 02 — [Make the smallest safe decision](smallest-safe-decision.md)
Decide only what we need to decide now.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 03 — [Design for change](design-for-change.md)
Keep decisions, components and technologies replaceable where possible.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 04 — [Use what exists — until it stops helping](use-what-exists.md)
Respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 05 — [Preserve future choice](preserve-future-choice.md)
Prefer openness, portability, interoperability and explicit trade-offs.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 06 — [Treat data as part of the service](data-as-part-of-service.md)
Understand ownership, movement, quality, semantics and value from the start.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 07 — [Build trust into the design](build-trust.md)
Security, privacy, accessibility, resilience and operability are designed in.
</div>
<div class="cf-card" markdown>
### 08 — [Prove rather than predict](prove-rather-than-predict.md)
Use evidence, experiments and observable behaviour rather than architectural opinion.
</div>
</div>
