# Bounded risk and exposure

“Small” is not just a technical measure.

Bound an experiment across the things that can create consequence:

- people and user groups
- data sensitivity and volume
- integrations and dependent systems
- financial or operational impact
- duration
- reversibility
- organisational commitments

<div class="cf-inset">A technically small experiment involving sensitive data can require more assurance than a technically complex experiment using synthetic data.</div>
