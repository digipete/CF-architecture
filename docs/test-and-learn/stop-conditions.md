# Stop conditions

Define in advance what would cause the experiment to pause or stop.

This may include:

- unacceptable harm or risk
- a critical assumption being disproven
- data quality below the level required
- inability to operate within agreed guardrails
- cost or complexity exceeding the hypothesis
- evidence that the intervention does not improve the outcome

Stopping based on evidence is a valid outcome. It prevents further investment in something that no longer has sufficient justification.
