# Before a Test & Learn Experiment

The question is not “has architecture approved this?”

The question is:

> **Is this bounded experiment safe enough and useful enough to run?**

## Check

- The decision being informed is clear.
- Material assumptions are visible.
- Exposure and potential harm are understood.
- Guardrails are proportionate.
- Evidence can actually be collected.
- Stop conditions are explicit.
- Ownership during the experiment is clear.

Do not require evidence that can only be produced by running the experiment itself.
