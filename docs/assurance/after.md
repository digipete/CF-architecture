# After a Test & Learn Experiment

The question becomes:

> **Is the evidence strong enough to support the decision we are about to make?**

## Possible outcomes

### Scale
Increase commitment or exposure because the evidence supports it.

### Test again
Important uncertainty remains and another bounded test can resolve it.

### Modify
The evidence supports continuing, but not in the current form.

### Stop
The evidence does not justify further investment or exposure.

Each outcome should identify what changed in our understanding and what the next decision requires.
