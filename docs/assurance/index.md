# Assurance

<p class="cf-lede">Safe enough to learn. Strong enough to decide.</p>

<div class="cf-inset cf-inset--green"><strong>Assurance is not approval of the eventual solution.</strong><br>It is confidence that we understand the risk of the next move.</div>

## Before the experiment

Ask whether the bounded experiment is safe enough and useful enough to run.

1. What decision are we trying to inform?
2. What assumptions or uncertainties matter?
3. What could cause harm?
4. What evidence do we need?
5. What guardrails are required?

[Before a Test & Learn Experiment →](before.md)

## After the experiment

Review the evidence and ask whether it is strong enough to support the decision we are about to make.

<div class="cf-outcomes"><div>SCALE</div><div>TEST AGAIN</div><div>MODIFY</div><div>STOP</div></div>

## Proportional assurance

Assurance increases with **exposure, consequence, irreversibility and scale** — not simply because work has reached another governance stage.
