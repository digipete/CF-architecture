# Partner environment assessment

<p class="cf-lede">Respect the partner environment without becoming trapped by it.</p>

CustomerFirst works within partner organisations' existing environments, technologies, standards and processes where they enable progress and support sustainable ownership.

Where those constraints materially prevent learning or delivery, CustomerFirst may explore alternative tools, environments or approaches.

Any departure should be:

- deliberate
- transparent
- proportionate
- understood
- appropriately de-risked

## Questions to ask

- What existing capability can we reuse?
- Which constraints are mandatory and which are convention?
- What specifically is preventing progress or learning?
- Can the constraint itself be tested or changed?
- If we work outside the existing environment, what new risk or dependency do we create?
- How will the partner ultimately own, operate or absorb the outcome?

<div class="cf-inset cf-inset--blue">CustomerFirst should leave partner organisations more capable of owning, operating and changing the service than when we arrived.</div>
