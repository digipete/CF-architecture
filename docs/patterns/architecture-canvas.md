# Architecture canvas

Use this as a one-page prompt when a team needs to shape the architectural part of a decision or experiment.

## Decision
What decision are we trying to inform?

## Outcome
What should be different if this works?

## Important assumptions
What are we relying on that we do not yet know?

## Domains that matter now
Which of the six architecture lenses could materially change the result?

## Experiment boundary
Who, what data, which systems, which environments and for how long?

## Guardrails
What must remain true for the experiment to be safe enough to run?

## Evidence
What will we measure, observe or prove?

## Stop conditions
What would tell us not to continue?

## Next decision
What will the evidence allow us to decide?
