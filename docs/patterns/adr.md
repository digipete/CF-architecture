# Architecture Decision Record

Use an ADR when an architectural decision is important enough that another person may later need to understand why it was made.

## Template

```markdown
# ADR: [Decision title]

## Context
What decision needs to be made and why now?

## Options
What realistic options did we consider?

## Evidence
What do we know? What remains uncertain?

## Decision
What have we decided for now?

## Consequences
What does this enable, constrain or defer?

## Review trigger
What new evidence, scale or change would cause us to revisit this decision?
```

Keep the record proportionate. An ADR is a memory aid, not an approval document.
